# Butler step 2: founder's wish list (2026-11-03, not built)

Agreed with the founder in chat; nothing here is designed or built yet. Build after Screen view round 2a.

## Core of step 2
- Click by name (Windows accessibility names): the phone shows what will be clicked; the owner taps Confirm.
- Typing action: types only the words the owner said into the focused box; never shortcuts, never Enter on its own.
- "Search Netflix" action (opens Netflix's search page in the browser, like youtube.search); check Netflix's terms first.
- Launch Microsoft Store apps (e.g. Netflix) after approval on the PC (today only .exe files can be approved).

## Help system (founder's idea)
- First-time intro with three or four example sentences.
- "Help" / "What can you do?": examples built by the phone from what is set up (approved apps); instant, free, no AI turn.
- When it cannot do something: say why and suggest a sentence that works.
- After two misunderstandings in a row: offer to show how to talk to it.
- "Talk to DoMe" cheat-sheet page in Help, grouped by topic.

## Other additions proposed
1. "Stop" / "cancel" cancels anything pending, any time.
2. Nicknames stored on the phone only ("my show" = Netflix; "movie mode" = Netflix full screen, volume 30).
3. Saved routines by voice ("movie night"), one confirm.
4. Sleep timer ("turn off the PC in 2 hours", "pause in 30 minutes") with a cancellable countdown.
5. Search actions for other services (Spotify, Disney+), each after checking that service's terms.
6. With Screen view open, highlight the click target on the phone before the owner confirms.

## Later, not part of step 2
- An App Store version of the phone app (wrapper around the PWA, Apple developer account, review, payment rules).
